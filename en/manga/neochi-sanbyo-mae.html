Three Seconds to Sleep

A 4-koma episode of Paguo the pug.

Dialogue in English

Panel 2

Paguo: ...I'm not asleep yet.

Panel 4

Three seconds later

Fighting sleep is hopeless. The last three seconds before Paguo dozes off.

Paguo is a pug character who is sleepy, lazy, and unable to say what he really feels — except that his tail always gives him away. New episodes are published in Japanese first and added here as they appear. The dialogue inside the artwork is in Japanese, so an English translation of every line is printed right under the comic.
